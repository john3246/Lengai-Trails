FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Tanzania safari package for a first visit?

Most first-time visitors choose five to seven days on the Northern Circuit, visiting Tarangire, the Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater.

How much does a Tanzania safari cost?

The price depends on the season, how comfortable you want the lodges to be and how many people share the vehicle. Each trip page shows a per-person starting price and what it covers.

When is the best time to go on safari in Tanzania?

June to October is the dry season and the easiest time to spot animals. From January to March the herds calve on the southern Serengeti plains.

Do you offer private safaris and joining group safaris?

Yes to both. Any route can be run privately, and set-date shared departures cost less per person.

Can I climb Kilimanjaro or visit Zanzibar as well as go on safari?

Yes, they combine well. Many guests add a Kilimanjaro climb or finish with a few days on Zanzibar.

Where is Lengai Trails Expeditions based?

Our office is in Arusha, in northern Tanzania, and our team is Tanzanian.

Do I need travel insurance for a Tanzania safari?

We strongly advise it. Choose a policy that covers medical treatment and emergency evacuation.
