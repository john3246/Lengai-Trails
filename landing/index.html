FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Tanzania safari package for a first visit?

A 5 to 7 day Northern Circuit safari covering Tarangire, Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater is the classic first-time choice.

How much does a Tanzania safari cost?

Prices depend on season, lodge level and group size. Our packages list a clear from-price per person with what is included.

When is the best time to go on safari in Tanzania?

Dry months from June to October are best for wildlife; the calving season runs January to March in the south of the Serengeti.

Do you offer private safaris and joining group safaris?

Yes. Every itinerary can be private, and fixed-date joining safaris are available for a lower per-person price.

Can I climb Kilimanjaro or visit Zanzibar as well as go on safari?

Yes. Many guests combine a safari with a Kilimanjaro climb or finish with a beach stay on Zanzibar.

Where is Lengai Trails Expeditions based?

We are a locally owned company based in Arusha, Tanzania.

Do I need travel insurance for a Tanzania safari?

Yes, we recommend comprehensive travel insurance that covers medical care and evacuation.
