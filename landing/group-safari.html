3 Day Affordable Joining Safari

Book this safariView open departures

What you get

Shared vehicle · Lodge nights

Three Northern Circuit parks

Tarangire, Ngorongoro Crater and Lake Manyara in three days.

Shared 4x4 and guide

A professional English-speaking guide and a well maintained safari vehicle.

Two nights in lodges

Lodge accommodation with meals as per the itinerary.

Fees included

Park and crater fees are included in the price.

Itinerary

Your three days

Day 1

Arusha to Tarangire

Morning pick-up, game drive among baobabs and elephant herds, overnight at a lodge.

Day 2

Lake Manyara & Ngorongoro

Early drive via Lake Manyara toward the crater highlands, overnight on the rim.

Day 3

Ngorongoro Crater

Descend into the crater for a full game drive, then return to Arusha.
