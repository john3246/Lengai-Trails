Tanzania destinations

The Northern Circuit for the classic Big Five, the south for wilderness without the crowds, and the coast for white sand and Swahili history.

Northern Tanzania

The Northern Circuit

Tanzania’s most popular safari route: huge herds, the world’s largest intact caldera and big-cat country, all within reach of Arusha.

  • SerengetiEndless plains and the Great Migration
  • Ngorongoro CraterA wildlife-packed volcanic caldera
  • TarangireGiant baobabs and elephant herds
  • Lake ManyaraTree-climbing lions and flamingos

Southern Tanzania

Wilderness without the crowds

Boat safaris, walking safaris and open bush with far fewer vehicles. Ideal for a second visit or a quieter trip.

  • Nyerere National ParkBoats, walks and the wild south
  • RuahaRugged country and big lion prides
  • MikumiEasy-access savanna close to Dar es Salaam

The Coast

Zanzibar & the Indian Ocean

End your safari with white sand beaches, the lanes of Stone Town and spice farms on Zanzibar.

  • Stone TownSwahili history and winding lanes
  • BeachesQuiet white sand and warm water
  • Spice farmsTaste and smell the Spice Island
