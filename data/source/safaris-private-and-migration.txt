5-Day Fly-In Serengeti Wildebeest Migration Safari from Arusha
OVERVIEW
Fly from Arusha straight into the heart of the Great Wildebeest Migration on this unforgettable 5-day Tanzania safari. Designed for travelers who want to witness the spectacular Mara River crossings without spending long hours on the road, this Fly-In Migration Safari takes you to Northern Serengeti during the peak migration season from July to October. Stay close to the action in carefully selected camps and enjoy thrilling game drives as thousands of wildebeest and zebras move through the Serengeti, alongside incredible opportunities to see lions, leopards, cheetahs, elephants, and other African wildlife. With scenic flights, prime migration locations, and unforgettable wildlife experiences, this safari offers an exciting and time-efficient way to experience the best of the Serengeti migration.
Itinerary
Day 1. Arusha to Northern Serengeti
Your safari adventure begins with a hotel pickup in Arusha and a transfer to Arusha Airport for a one-hour flight to Kogatende Airstrip in Northern Serengeti. Upon arrival, your private safari vehicle will be ready for an exciting Northern Serengeti game drive, with a visit to the Mara River to witness the spectacular Great Wildebeest Migration crossing.
From July to October, the Mara River in Northern Serengeti becomes the dramatic stage of the Great Migration. Wildebeest gather along the riverbanks before making the plunge, while crocodiles wait in the water and predators such as lions and hyenas patrol nearby.
You’ll spend time around Kogatende or Lamai, giving you a better opportunity to experience a river crossing when nature decides the moment is right. Between crossings, explore the quieter side of Northern Serengeti, where you may encounter lions on the kopjes, elephants in the Mara woodland, and fewer safari vehicles.
After an unforgettable day of wildlife and migration viewing, return to Safari Haven Migration Camp, a mobile migration camp ideally positioned in Northern Serengeti for the Mara River crossings, for dinner and a relaxing overnight stay.
Acc: Moyo Migration camp 
Meals:Lunch & Dinner
Day 2: Full Day Game Drive - Northern Serengeti
Wake up early for an unforgettable Northern Serengeti game drive, with the main goal of witnessing the spectacular Mara River crossings of the Great Wildebeest Migration. From July to October, thousands of wildebeest and zebras gather along the Mara River, creating one of Africa’s most dramatic wildlife experiences.
Explore the Kogatende and Lamai areas, where you may encounter large herds, lions, elephants, leopards, and other Serengeti wildlife. After an exciting day in the north, enjoy a scenic game drive toward Central Serengeti, arriving at your camp in the evening.
Overnight: Moyo tented camp
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day: 3 Full-Day Game Drive in Central Serengeti
Wake up in the heart of the Central Serengeti and enjoy a full day game drive through the legendary Seronera Valley, one of Tanzania’s premier wildlife viewing areas. Known for its year-round abundance of wildlife, Central Serengeti offers excellent opportunities to encounter lions, leopards, cheetahs, elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeest and other plains game across its stunning open savannah.
Start early to catch predators when they are most active, then continue exploring the Seronera River, rocky kopjes and acacia-dotted plains. Enjoy a picnic lunch in the wilderness before continuing your afternoon Serengeti safari game drive, searching for big cats resting around the kopjes and leopards in the riverine areas.
As the day comes to an end, return to your accommodation for dinner and a relaxing evening surrounded by the atmosphere of the Serengeti.
Overnight: Moyo Tented Camp 
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 4: Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater
After breakfast, begin your journey from the Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater with an exciting transfer game drive through the beautiful Tanzanian wilderness. Enjoy approximately three hours of wildlife viewing along the way, with opportunities to spot zebras, wildebeest, giraffes, elephants and other wildlife.
Around 10:00 AM, descend into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater, a UNESCO World Heritage landscape and one of Tanzania’s most remarkable wildlife destinations. The 260 km² crater floor is home to an extraordinary concentration of wildlife, including lions, elephants, buffaloes, hippos, flamingos and the rare eastern black rhino.
Spend approximately five hours on a Ngorongoro Crater game drive, exploring the crater floor and its diverse habitats. Visit the soda lake, search for flamingos, explore the Lerai Forest and keep your eyes open for predators and large herds of plains game. After an unforgettable wildlife experience, ascend the crater and continue to your lodge for dinner and a relaxing overnight stay.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

After breakfast, depart Arusha for a scenic 90-minute drive to Tarangire National Park, one of Tanzania’s most spectacular wildlife destinations. Known for its impressive elephant herds, ancient baobab trees and diverse landscapes, Tarangire offers an unforgettable Tanzania safari experience.
Enjoy a full-day game drive through the park’s baobab-studded savannah, seasonal swamps and the Tarangire River. During the dry season, the river becomes a vital water source, attracting large herds of elephants and other wildlife. Keep your eyes open for lions, leopards, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, antelopes and a remarkable variety of bird species.
Enjoy a hot picnic lunch in the wilderness before continuing your Tarangire safari game drive, making the most of the afternoon light and abundant wildlife. After an exciting day exploring Tarangire National Park, begin your drive back to Arusha, arriving in the late afternoon and marking the end of your safari adventure.
